## The pattern

Organisations rarely decide to fragment their identity infrastructure. They accumulate it. Each individual decision is defensible — this team needs this tool, and the tool comes with its own accounts — and the aggregate is a set of overlapping access lists that no single person can describe.

## Why the cost is invisible for years

Nothing about this is expensive at first. The cost appears at the points where the whole has to be reasoned about at once: an employee leaving, an audit, an incident, an acquisition.

## What changes with consolidation

Consolidation does not remove the work of deciding who may do what. It removes the work of asking that question separately in every system, and of discovering that the answers had drifted apart.
