## A decision made once is a decision made everywhere

In a single product, a design decision can be revisited cheaply. Across a portfolio it cannot — by the time a pattern is wrong, it is wrong everywhere at once and has been learned by every customer.

## The discipline that follows

The practical effect is that fewer decisions get made, and the ones that do get made take longer. That reads as slowness from inside a single team and as coherence from outside.
