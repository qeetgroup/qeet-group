## The problem it solves

Most organisations discover they cannot answer basic questions about their own systems at precisely the moment being unable to answer them is expensive: during an incident, during an audit, during a conversation with a customer who wants to know what happened to their data.

## How Qeet approaches it

Collect what systems record about themselves, keep it in one place, and make it searchable when somebody needs it — which is almost always when nobody has time to go looking.

There is a second problem discussed far less often. Operational data is full of personal information nobody deliberately decided to store; it arrives incidentally and accumulates. Removing sensitive detail before it is written down, rather than after, means an organisation is not quietly building a liability while trying to build visibility.
