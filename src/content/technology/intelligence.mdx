## The problem it solves

Organisations adopting artificial intelligence meet the same wall in the same order. Obtaining the capability is easy. Demonstrating it is easy. Connecting it safely to the organisation's own information is the hard part, and it is the only part that produces value past the demonstration.

## How Qeet approaches it

The constraint is the product. Anything done on someone's behalf is bounded by what that person is actually permitted to see — the same boundary that governs every other Qeet product, because it comes from the same place.

A system that cannot see what the organisation knows is a novelty. One that can see all of it, regardless of who is asking, is a liability that will eventually be discovered. The useful position is the narrow one between those two, and it is only reachable if identity was solved first.

## What this is not

It is not an assistant, and it is not a wrapper around someone else's model. It composes the rest of the portfolio rather than replacing any of it.
