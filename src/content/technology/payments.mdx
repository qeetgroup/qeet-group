## The problem it solves

Taking payments is the visible half. The other half — invoicing that satisfies a tax authority, books that balance, payouts that reconcile — is where the work actually is, and where most businesses discover their payment provider stops.

## How Qeet approaches it

Designed around how money moves in India rather than adapted to it afterwards. Most payment infrastructure is built elsewhere and localised later, and the seam always shows: the payment method most customers here prefer arrives last, and compliant invoicing is treated as somebody else's problem.

Building for those requirements from the start produces a narrower product, and for organisations operating here, a considerably more useful one.
