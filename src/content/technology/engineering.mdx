## Shared foundations, independent products

Each product owns its own architecture and makes its own technical decisions. What is not optional is the connective layer: identity, the design foundation, notifications and operational visibility are composed, not rebuilt.

Where a product does not yet compose one of them, that is recorded as a gap rather than treated as licence to build a parallel version of something that already exists.

## Evidence over assertion

The organisation keeps a register of the places its own documentation disagrees with its code, and updates the documentation rather than quietly asserting it was right.

The rule that follows is the one that shapes this website: a roadmap entry may not be cited as a shipped capability. It is why the portfolio here distinguishes what is available from what is being built from what is merely planned, and why that distinction is enforced by the code that renders these pages rather than left to whoever writes the copy.

## What we do not do

We do not ship a capability and describe it as finished because the demo worked. We do not claim certifications we do not hold. We do not present a planned product as something you could buy today.
