## What it does

Software produces an enormous quantity of description about its own behaviour. Qeet Logs gathers it, keeps it in one place, and makes it searchable at the moment somebody needs it — which is almost always during an incident, when nobody has time to go looking.

It answers the questions that follow a failure: what happened, when, to whom, and what changed just before.

## Why it matters

Most organisations discover they cannot answer those questions at exactly the point where being unable to answer them is expensive.

There is a second problem that is discussed less. Operational data is full of personal information that nobody deliberately decided to store — it arrives incidentally, and it accumulates. Qeet Logs removes sensitive detail before it is written down rather than after, which means the organisation is not quietly building a liability while trying to build visibility.

## Where it fits

Qeet Logs is where the rest of the ecosystem reports what it is doing. It is also the product that makes the others accountable: when a question is asked about what a system did, this is where the answer comes from.
