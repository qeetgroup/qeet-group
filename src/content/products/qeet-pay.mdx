## What it does

Qeet Pay covers the movement of money through a business: collecting it, paying it out, invoicing for it, and keeping a record that balances.

It is built around the ways money actually moves in India — instant transfers, cards, bank mandates, payouts — and around the invoicing and tax obligations that come with them.

## Why it matters

Most payment infrastructure is designed elsewhere and adapted for India afterwards. The adaptation is usually visible: the local payment method that most customers prefer is supported last and least, and tax-compliant invoicing is treated as somebody else's problem.

Designing for those requirements from the beginning produces a different product. It is a narrower one, and for organisations operating here, a considerably more useful one.

## Where it fits

Qeet Pay is intended to be where the rest of the ecosystem bills from, so that an organisation buying several Qeet products has one commercial relationship rather than several.
