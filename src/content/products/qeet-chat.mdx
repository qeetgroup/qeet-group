## What it will do

Team messaging where retention is a decision the organisation makes rather than a default it inherits, and where conversations can be found later by the people entitled to find them.

## Where it fits

Part of the planned productivity suite — a set of everyday working tools that share one identity, one design foundation and one set of controls, so that an organisation administers them together rather than separately.

## Status

Planned. Specified, not yet built. There is nothing to try.
