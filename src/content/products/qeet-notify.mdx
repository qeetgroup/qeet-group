## What it does

Every organisation sends messages it cannot afford to lose: a one-time code, a payment confirmation, an alert that something needs attention. Qeet Notify sends them across email, SMS, messaging apps, push and in-app channels through one system, and keeps track of whether they actually arrived.

Where a message fails, it retries. Where it cannot be delivered at all, it says so, rather than failing silently.

## Why it matters

Transactional messaging looks trivial until it is examined. It is subject to real regulation — India in particular has strict rules about what may be sent, to whom, and at what hours — and the penalties for getting it wrong are not theoretical.

Most organisations end up with several partial answers: one service for email, another for text messages, a third bolted on for app notifications, and no single view of whether anything reached anyone. Consolidating that is worth doing once.

## Where it fits

When any Qeet product needs to tell someone something, it does so through Qeet Notify. That is why a notification from one product looks and behaves like a notification from another, and why an organisation can set its communication preferences in one place.
