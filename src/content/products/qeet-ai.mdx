## What it does

Qeet AI is the part of the ecosystem that lets software reason: to remember what has happened before, to draw on what the organisation knows, and to act on it.

The distinguishing constraint is permission. Anything Qeet AI does on someone's behalf is bounded by what that person is actually allowed to see — the same boundary Qeet ID enforces everywhere else.

## Why it matters

Most organisations adopting artificial intelligence discover the same problem in the same order. The capability is easy to obtain and easy to demonstrate. Connecting it to the organisation's own information, safely, is the hard part — and it is the only part that produces value beyond the demonstration.

An intelligent system that cannot see the organisation's knowledge is a novelty. One that can see all of it, regardless of who is asking, is a serious risk. The useful position is the narrow one between those, and reaching it depends on identity, which is why this product is built where it is rather than bought separately.

## Where it fits

Qeet AI composes the rest of the ecosystem rather than replacing any of it. It is deliberately built on top of identity rather than beside it.
